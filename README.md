## About the Data

The dataset is designed to be a perfect beginner's sandbox for ML. It includes a mix of:
* **Categorical Data:** `Name`, `Race`, `Saga`, `Alignment`
* **Numerical Data:** `BasePowerLevel`, `MaxPowerLevel`, `Age`
* **Boolean Data:** `HasKiControl`

This structure makes it ideal for practicing a variety of models.

## 🚀 Inspiration & ML Tasks (Your Mission!)

Here are a few questions you can try to answer with this dataset.

* **Regression (Predicting Numbers):**
    * Can you build a model to predict a character's `MaxPowerLevel` based on their `BasePowerLevel`, `Race`, and `Saga`?
    * Can you predict a character's (nonsensical) `Age`?

* **Classification (Predicting Categories):**
    * This is the fun one: Can you train a model to predict a character's `Race` based on their `Name` and `Saga`?
    * Can you predict a character's `Alignment` (`Hero`, `Villain`, etc.) based on their power levels and race?

* **Clustering (Finding Groups):**
    * If you ignore the labels, can a K-Means or DBSCAN algorithm find "natural" clusters of characters? (e.g., a "Z-Fighter" cluster, a "Villain" cluster).

Good luck, and have fun!
